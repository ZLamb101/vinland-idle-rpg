## Class Identity Summary

### Plate (Tank)
- **Strength**: Highest survivability (900+ HP, 49% damage reduction)
- **Weakness**: Lowest damage output, slowest kills
- **Playstyle**: Stay alive through heavy armor and health pool

### Leather (Physical DPS)
- **Strength**: Balanced offense/defense, high crit and dodge
- **Weakness**: Medium survivability
- **Playstyle**: Dodge attacks, land critical hits

### Cloth (Glass Cannon/Utility)
- **Strength**: Highest damage, XP/gold/AFK bonuses
- **Weakness**: Very squishy (161 HP, minimal armor)
- **Playstyle**: Kill fast before dying, benefit from utility bonuses

## Design Notes

1. **Armor percentages** are balanced so full plate reaches ~50% (meaningful but not invincible)
2. **Crit chance** caps around 35-40% for cloth to remain impactful without being guaranteed
3. **Dodge** is primarily a leather stat, reaching ~25-30% for tactical avoidance
4. **Utility stats** (XP/gold/AFK) are exclusive to cloth as trade-off for low survivability
5. **Lifesteal** is rare (only on level 20 weapons) as a premium stat
